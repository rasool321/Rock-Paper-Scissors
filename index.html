Game Rules:

  • ✋ **Paper beats Rock**
  • ✌ **Scissors beats Paper**
  • If both players choose the same, it's a **Tie**.

How to Play:

Simply choose **Rock**, **Paper**, or **Scissors**, and the computer will randomly select one too. The winner is decided based on the rules above.
